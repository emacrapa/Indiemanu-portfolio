I make games.
I break games.

Hi! I'm Emanuele — a Game Designer turned QA Tester with a passion for crafting experiences that stick. With over 3 years in the industry, I've shipped AAA motorcycle titles at Milestone, led indie teams from jam to release, and studied the craft at Event Horizon School.

I believe good games come from obsessing over details, communicating clearly, and being willing to scrap your own darlings. Whether I'm drafting a GDD or filing a P1 bug, I'm always chasing the same thing: a game worth playing.
